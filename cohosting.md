## 1. What is Vinclo Real Estate?

Vinclo Real Estate is a US-based short-term rental company built on two complementary business lines:

**Line A — Vinclo Management (co-hosting / third-party property management)**
We manage other owners' properties as short-term rentals. We don't own these properties. The owner keeps the asset (and, depending on the agreement, control of accounts and income); Vinclo runs the operation in exchange for a commission on revenue.

**Line B — Vinclo Arbitrage (STR rental arbitrage)**
We lease units directly from landlords and operate them ourselves as short-term rentals (Airbnb/VRBO). Here Vinclo carries the leasing risk and keeps the full spread between what we pay the landlord and what we earn from guests.

These two lines are not separate businesses run in parallel — they are meant to reinforce each other:

- Arbitrage builds hands-on operating experience, cash flow, and case studies that make the management pitch to owners more credible.
- Landlord relationships built through arbitrage are a sourcing channel for management clients (a landlord who leases us one unit may own others they'd rather have professionally managed than lease out).
- Management clients and their networks (realtors, investors, property managers) are a sourcing channel for arbitrage opportunities (off-market units, motivated landlords).
- Both lines can eventually share the same operational backbone: guest communication, pricing engine, cleaning/vendor network, calendar and reservations tooling, and reporting — so growth in one line doesn't require rebuilding infrastructure for the other.

I want every recommendation to consider which line (or both) it applies to, and how it can strengthen the other.

## 7. Revenue model

**Management:** commission on booking revenue generated by the property. Initial reference point: approximately **20% of booking revenue** for full-service management. Not fixed — can vary by property, market, volume (owners with multiple properties may get a negotiated rate), or scope (a-la-carte services at a flat fee or lower percentage). May also charge an onboarding/setup fee, and bill extraordinary additional services separately.

Example: a property generating $5,000/month at a 20% commission produces $1,000/month for Vinclo. 5 properties ≈ $5,000/month, 10 ≈ $10,000/month, 20 ≈ $20,000/month (illustrative only — depends on actual per-property revenue).

**Arbitrage:** the spread between what we pay the landlord in lease payments and what we collect from short-term guests, net of operating costs (cleaning, utilities, furnishing amortization, platform fees, etc.). This line carries more upfront capital exposure (deposits, furnishing, lease commitments) and more downside risk than Management, so deal discipline (see Section 6) matters more here than volume of leads.

The overall goal is to build recurring monthly income across both lines — management fees that scale without leasing risk, and arbitrage spread that scales portfolio value and operating expertise — without needing to buy real estate outright.

## 9. Sales / deal process

**Management sales process:**
1. **Quick response** — personalized, showing genuine interest in the property.
2. **Qualification** — location, property type, bedrooms, current operating status and approximate revenue, current problems, owner goals, services needed, vendor availability, market regulatory situation.
3. **Discovery call** — understand the owner's problem first, then present Vinclo as the solution. Not just a pitch.
4. **Property analysis** — market, competition, rates, estimated occupancy, listing quality, reviews, photos, amenities, improvement opportunities. Never promise guaranteed income.
5. **Proposal** — services included, Vinclo's responsibilities, owner's responsibilities, commission, additional costs, onboarding process, next steps.
6. **Contract** — signed professional agreement before management starts.
7. **Onboarding** — access credentials, property info, contacts, vendors, inventory, rules, emergency info, permits, insurance info, operational info.
8. **Launch or transition** — new property: prepare and launch the listing; existing operation: organized transition without disrupting existing bookings.

**Arbitrage deal process:**
1. **Sourcing** — RPA bot and direct outreach identify candidate units/landlords.
2. **Unit qualification** — confirm subletting is allowed, check local STR regulations, gather unit and market details.
3. **Financial analysis** — run the property/deal analyzer (P&L sliders, AirDNA benchmarks) under conservative and base-case assumptions.
4. **Landlord negotiation** — lease terms, deposit, subletting permissions, term length.
5. **Lease signing.**
6. **Setup** — furnishing, listing creation, smart locks, supplies, local vendor lineup.
7. **Launch** — go live across the relevant platforms (Airbnb/VRBO).
